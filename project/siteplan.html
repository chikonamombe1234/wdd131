Discover Kyoto

A friendly starting point for visitors planning a trip to Kyoto, Japan. This plan outlines the intended content, visual style, and home-page layout.

Site Name

Discover Kyoto

“Discover Kyoto” is a clear, memorable name for a site introducing visitors to the city. It suggests exploring Kyoto’s landmarks, traditions, food, and practical travel information.

Proposed domain: discover-kyoto.example

The example domain is a placeholder; domain registration is not required for this assignment.

Site Purpose

The site will help first-time visitors learn about Kyoto and plan a rewarding visit. It will provide a brief city introduction, suggestions for notable places to visit, cultural and food highlights, and practical travel tips. The home page will use clear sections and links to make information easy to find on both mobile and desktop screens.

Scenarios

Questions a visitor to the site might ask include:

  • Which well-known Kyoto landmarks should I visit during a short trip?
  • What local foods should I try while I am in Kyoto?
  • How can I get around the city using public transportation?
  • What customs should I know before visiting temples and shrines?

Color Schema

The palette pairs deep indigo and warm ivory with a vermilion accent, drawing inspiration from traditional Japanese design. The page background and body text use high-contrast colors for comfortable reading.

Indigo #203A5A Header, headings, and footer
Warm ivory #F7F3EA Page background and light panels
Vermilion #B84432 Links, buttons, and small accents
Charcoal #252525 Paragraph text on light backgrounds

Typography

The site plan uses two typefaces. Lora is used for headings to give them a distinctive, editorial feel. Open Sans is used for body copy and interface text because it is easy to read at different screen sizes.

  • Lora: page title and section headings
  • Open Sans: paragraphs, navigation, labels, and buttons
